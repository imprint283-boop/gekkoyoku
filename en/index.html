Our specialties

Hakata jidori yakitori

Grilled one skewer at a time over charcoal. Try the chef's choice 5-skewer set (¥1,320).

Motsunabe (beef offal hot pot)

Domestic beef offal sent directly from Hokkaido. Three flavors: soy sauce with ago-dashi (flying fish stock), miso, and spicy miso. Finish with ramen noodles or rice porridge. From 2 people.

Hakata jidori mizutaki

A rich soup made by simmering Hakata jidori chicken bones for two days, with chunks of chicken and handmade meatballs. Finish with tantanmen noodles or rice porridge. From 3 people; please reserve by the day before.

Fully private rooms

Private table rooms and horigotatsu rooms (where you sit on the floor with a well for your legs). Both are fully private, so you can relax without worrying about other guests.

Banquets and groups

A banquet floor that can be booked for private hire. Parties of up to 70 people can be arranged. For group bookings, we can also discuss opening outside regular hours or on our closing day.

FAQ

Do you have private rooms?

Yes. We have private table rooms and horigotatsu rooms, and both are fully private.

How many people can you take for a party?

Parties of up to 70 people can be arranged, and the banquet floor can be booked for private hire.

Do I need to reserve mizutaki?

Yes. Mizutaki is for 3 or more people and must be reserved by the day before.

What are your opening hours?

18:00–24:00 (last order 23:30). Closed on Sundays, except Sundays before a public holiday. Please check Google Maps for the latest opening days.
